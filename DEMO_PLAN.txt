OXMARKET DEMO PLAN  (target: 7-8 minutes + questions)
=====================================================

One-line pitch
--------------
OxMarket is a play-money prediction market on Oxford life: college Cuppers matches,
Oxford Union questions and Oxford news. Each outcome is its own YES/NO market, and a price
in pence is the market's probability (62p = 62% chance). The hackathon theme is TIME, so
the TimescaleDB-backed Time Machine can replay what the market believed at any moment.


BEFORE THE DEMO (do this 15+ minutes early)
-------------------------------------------
1. Build the C++ engine (book/OrderBook, target MarketServer) so that
   book/OrderBook/build/MarketServer.exe exists. Without it the server falls back to the
   pure-Python book. That works, but the demo should run on the real engine.
2. In .env set DISABLE_JOBS=0. This turns on the house market maker, price ticks,
   auto-close, portfolio snapshots and the news scraper. Without it the order books and
   charts stay static.
3. Optional: set GEMINI_API_KEY. Without it the "Why did the odds move?" briefs and the
   digest are fixed placeholder text, and no AI-drafted markets appear from news articles.
4. Start the server:  python server/app.py   ->  http://localhost:5000
   Check the log says the engine started (no "pure-Python fallback" error).
5. Accounts:
   - "admin" (the name listed in ADMIN_USERNAMES) for the admin portal.
   - A normal trader, e.g. "demo_trader", registered in advance. Place a few trades with it
     so the portfolio and leaderboard have something to show.
6. Leave the jobs running for ~10 minutes so the charts, Biggest movers and Time Machine
   have recent history.
7. Open these tabs in order:
     1  /                  (home)
     2  /login.html        (logged in as demo_trader)
     3  /admin.html        (in a second browser or private window, logged in as admin)
     4  /timemachine.html
8. Have a Cuppers match ready that you can settle live: a market whose close time has
   passed, so it shows under Admin > Markets > Awaiting result. (Or create one before the
   demo with a close time a few minutes later.)


DEMO SCRIPT
-----------

1. HOME PAGE  /                                                            (~1 min)
   - "Trade on Cuppers matches, Oxford Union questions and Oxford news."
   - Explain the core idea: price = probability. 62p means the crowd thinks 62%.
   - Tabs: Upcoming / Awaiting result / Results. The search box filters matches.
   - Biggest movers: the largest odds changes in the last 24 hours.
   - Latest Cuppers news: scraped from the Cherwell and Oxford Student RSS feeds.
   - Point out the three market types on the page: Cuppers matches (Home/Draw/Away),
     Yes/No questions (e.g. the Oxford Union markets) and multiple choice.

2. SIGN UP / LOG IN  /login.html                                           (~20 s)
   - Anyone can sign up. Usernames are 3-20 characters, passwords at least 6.
   - Every new account starts with GBP 1,000 of play money. No real money is involved.
   - Optionally sign up a fresh account live to show how quick it is.

3. MATCH PAGE  /race.html  (click a Cuppers match)                         (~1 min)
   - Odds over time chart with 1m / 5m / 1h buckets (stored in TimescaleDB).
   - Outcomes table: chance, 24h change and 24h volume per outcome, with a Trade button.
   - The outcomes of a match are normalised so their chances add up sensibly.
   - "Why did the odds move?": a Gemini-generated explanation, with Refresh analysis.
   - Rules section: how the market resolves.

4. TRADING  /market.html  (click Trade on one outcome)                     (~2 min)
   This is the centrepiece, so go slowly.
   - Order book: real bids and asks, in YES terms with the NO equivalent greyed out.
     Clicking a level fills the order ticket.
   - The house market maker keeps both sides quoted, so there is always someone to trade
     with. It quotes around a fair price: the AI prior until the first trade, then an
     average of recent trades.
   - Ticket: Buy Yes / Buy No, Limit or Market order, price in pence, quick share buttons
     (+10 / +50 / +100).
   - LIVE: Buy 50 YES at market. Show the fill appear in Recent trades and the chart move.
   - LIVE: Place a resting limit order below the market. It appears in the order book and
     under "Your open orders here". Cancel it.
   - Talking points:
     * NO is a view on the same book: Buy NO at p = Sell YES at (100 - p). One book per
       outcome, no separate NO liquidity.
     * Matching runs in a C++ order book engine (AVL tree of price levels) that the Python
       server drives over stdin/stdout.
     * Cash and positions live in Postgres. Every fill is written in a single transaction.
       If that write fails, the engine restarts and replays the open orders from the DB.
     * Risk check before an order reaches the engine: buys lock price x qty, shorts lock
       100 per share. Orders you can't cover are rejected. (Optional: try an order bigger
       than your balance to show the rejection.)

5. PORTFOLIO  /portfolio.html                                              (~30 s)
   - Positions grouped by match, valued at current prices.
   - Open orders, with cancel.
   - Cash vs. equity (cash plus open positions at current prices).

6. LEADERBOARD  /leaderboard.html                                          (~30 s)
   - Ranked by equity, with profit/loss against the starting GBP 1,000.
   - "Hindsight" chart: the equity of the current top five over the last week, built from
     the portfolio snapshots job.

7. TIME MACHINE  /timemachine.html          (the TIME theme, so make it land)  (~1.5 min)
   - "Replay the term as it happened."
   - Drag the slider back through Michaelmas. Every match's odds bars change to what the
     market believed at that moment, and the favourites re-sort.
   - Press play. Show the 1x / 3x / 10x speeds. The diamond marks a new favourite.
   - "Headlines as of then": the news that was published by that moment, next to the odds.
   - Click Now to jump back to the present.
   - Talking point: prices are stored in a TimescaleDB hypertable with compression, so
     "what were the odds at time T?" is a fast query, not a separate snapshot table.

8. ADMIN PORTAL  /admin.html  (switch to the admin window)                 (~2 min)
   - Markets tab:
     * Drafts awaiting approval (including AI-proposed markets): approve or reject.
     * LIVE: Awaiting result. Pick the winner of the prepared match and post the result.
       Positions settle and pay out straight away. Switch to the trader's Portfolio and
       Leaderboard to show the payout.
     * All markets, filterable by status, type and category: edit or stop trading.
   - Create market (LIVE, quick): show the three kinds.
     * Cuppers match: term, competition, round, home/away teams, whether a draw is possible.
     * Yes / No question: question text and opening chance of YES.
     * Multiple choice: named outcomes with opening odds, and the number of winners.
     * Set a close time (trading stops then). Publish now or save as a draft.
   - News articles: scraped headlines. Turn one into a market linked to its source, or
     dismiss it. With Gemini on, new articles automatically get draft markets.
   - Reference data: terms, categories, competitions, teams and sports, all editable.
   - Settings: market maker size, spread and interval, job intervals, starting cash and
     default odds. Changes apply within ~30 s with no restart.
   - Activity: every admin write is logged to an audit hypertable.

9. WRAP-UP                                                                 (~30 s)
   - Recap: real order book engine + Postgres ledger + TimescaleDB history + Gemini
     content, on markets Oxford students actually care about.
   - Next steps: more colleges and sports, live score feeds, automatic resolution
     from news.


FALLBACKS IF SOMETHING BREAKS
-----------------------------
- Engine exe missing or crashing: the server uses the pure-Python book with the same
  protocol. Keep going and mention it.
- No Gemini key / no network: briefs show placeholder text. Say that it is Gemini-backed
  and move on.
- Charts flat: the background jobs are off (DISABLE_JOBS=1). Make a few trades by hand
  so there is history.
- Login problems: have a second pre-registered trader account ready.


ARCHITECTURE CHEAT SHEET (for questions)
----------------------------------------
- Frontend: static HTML/CSS/JS in web/, served by Flask.
- Backend: Flask (server/app.py) with trading_api, data_api, admin_api and content_api
  blueprints.
- Matching: C++ OrderBook (book/OrderBook) as MarketServer.exe, driven by server/engine.py.
- Ledger: Postgres (schema oxmarket). trading.place_order does validate -> risk check ->
  engine -> write fills in one transaction.
- Time series: TimescaleDB hypertables for price history and admin actions.
- Jobs (server/jobs.py): market maker (mm_bot.py), price ticks, auto-close, portfolio
  snapshots, news scraper (scraper.py).
- AI (server/ai.py): Gemini match briefs, daily digest, markets drafted from articles.
